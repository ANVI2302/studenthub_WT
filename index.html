About CHARUSAT

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is one of Gujarat's leading universities offering Engineering, Pharmacy, Management, Computer Applications, Nursing, Applied Sciences and many other professional courses.

The university focuses on innovation, research, practical learning and overall personality development of students.
